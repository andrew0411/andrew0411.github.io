# AI Trends in 2025

As we progress through 2025, several AI trends have emerged:

- **Generative AI Everywhere:** From text to image and video generation.
- **AI in Healthcare:** More adoption in diagnostics and drug discovery.
- **Edge AI:** Running AI models on devices (smartphones, IoT) is on the rise.

These trends indicate a broadening impact of AI across industries.
